_=+¯ _=+¯ _=⭷ EFFECTS ⭹+¯ _=+¯ _=+¯
● ⚡️ Shock - 50% Chance that bullet won't shoot. If effect is triggered then it has 50% chance that effect won't disappear (works only once)
● ☂️ Blindness - Enemy can't see their ammo bar and ammo runs out faster. 50% chance that you lose 2 bullets instead of 1 per each shot
● ❄️ Freezing - (does not slow down enemies lol)
Enemies have to hit the reload button (Q/R) 3 times to finally reload. The more effects - the more times they have to hit the button (+1 for each freezing effect)
● ♨️ Burn - Takes 3 Damage every dash. Nothing can disable this effect. For each effect enemies will lose more health every time they dash
● ⚗️ Poison - Takes 2 damage after 2 seconds.
Nothing can disable effect. For each effect enemies will lose more health over time
